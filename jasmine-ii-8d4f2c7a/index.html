Source noteContext and limitations retained from the original plate
This viewer is an interactive reversed-orientation presentation derived from page 15 of the binder PDF. The marked option diagrams, branded header, and footer were removed. The drawing was then mirrored across the vertical axis, the third tandem garage bay was replaced with the builder’s optional bedroom, bath, and hall at garage (shown on the source page as Bedroom 6 w/Bath 4), renumbered here as Bedroom 5 and Bath 3 to match this house, and the base covered patio was replaced with Covered Patio Extension #2. The powder room and coat closet were dropped; the storage closet under the stairs remains. Option geometry was composited at 1:1 scale from the same page. The source page states that the plan is preliminary, artist-conceived, not intended to show specific detailing, and subject to change. As-built conditions control; dimensions and maps are approximate and not to scale.
