Good first-test examples:
- Turn one approved research packet into a cited status brief.
- Convert one transcript into decisions, actions, and unknowns.
- Compare two approved protocol versions and list changed sections.
- Draft an evidence plan before starting a Researcher investigation.

Avoid as a first test:
- Unbounded access to sensitive participant data.
- Automatic approval, sending, publishing, or record changes.
- A workflow with no source owner or review step.
