FICTIONAL AGENT CANDIDATES

A. Study Library Navigator
Answers recurring questions about approved protocols, templates, and study-status files in one governed SharePoint library. Must cite files and respect permissions. No actions.

B. Final Claims Approver
Reads draft study results and automatically approves consumer-facing product claims. Would require scientific, legal, regulatory, and brand authority.

C. Weekly Evidence Briefing Assistant
Creates a draft weekly summary from a defined SharePoint folder. Separates new documents, changed decisions, risks, and missing evidence. Human reviews before distribution.

D. One-Time Meeting Recap
Summarizes a single supplied transcript for one project checkpoint.

E. Participant Data Explorer
Answers unrestricted questions across row-level participant records, including sensitive data, without a defined access model or approved purpose.

Scoring criteria:
- Repeats often
- Has a stable audience and job
- Has a bounded, maintained knowledge source
- Can cite evidence
- Has clear human review
- Avoids authority it should not have
- Has an owner who can test and maintain it
