What this number is and is not

Microsoft passes AB-100 at 700 on a scaled 1–1000 range, says that may not equal 70%, and does not publish the conversion. The 80% ready band and 70% borderline band are this site’s study heuristic — a safety margin, not an official score or prediction.

Exam timer
