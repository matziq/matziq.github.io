What this number is and is not

Microsoft has never published its raw-to-scaled conversion, and the real exam weights harder questions more heavily. This curve is anchored so that 70% correct lands on 700, which is where the passing mark sits. Treat it as a readiness signal with a margin of error of roughly ±50 points, not as a prediction.

Error log

The single highest-value habit in the whole plan. Every question you miss goes in here — not the question, the rule. “Object detection returns a bounding box; classification labels the whole image” is a log entry. “Missed question 14” is not.

Tag the cause, because the three causes have three different fixes

Never knew it means learn the fact — flashcards. Knew it and read past it means slow down and read the last line first — that is a pacing and care problem, and more studying will not touch it. Misread the question means you missed a BEST, a FIRST, or a NOT — that is a reading habit, and it is the cheapest one to fix.
