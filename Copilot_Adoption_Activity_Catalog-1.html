BU / Function Training

Function-specific sessions built around the real workflows of one team. Each opens with a short foundations recap, walks through function use cases with live demos, and closes with next steps. Typical length is 60-90 minutes, and a function leader or champion validates scenarios in advance. The same pattern extends to other teams such as Sales and IT.
