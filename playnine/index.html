Rules, at a glance

A quick reference in original wording. Check your game’s official instructions if a table question comes up.

Object of the game

Play with 2 to 6 people. Each hand is one hole, and the game lasts nine holes. Add each player’s hole scores; the lowest final total wins.

Cards and values

The 108-card deck has eight cards of each value from 0 through 12, plus four Hole-in-One cards worth -5. The 0 card is called Mulligan, and 12 is Out of Bounds.

Choose a dealer and set up

Everyone draws a card to choose the first dealer. The lowest value deals; redraw tied draws. Deal eight cards face down to each player, who lays them out in two rows of four. Put the rest in a draw pile and turn its top card face up to start the discard pile. The dealer’s left-hand neighbor takes the first turn; play moves clockwise.

Teeing off

Before play begins, each player turns over any two of their own cards. This is the only time a player turns over two cards at once.

Take a turn

Choose the top card from either pile:

  • Draw pile: swap the drawn card with any one of your eight cards, face up or down, and discard the card you replaced; or discard the drawn card, then turn one of your face-down cards face up.
  • Discard pile: use that card to replace one of your cards, face up or down, then discard the replaced card.

Usually you must replace a card or turn over a face-down card. You can keep swapping face-up cards while at least one of your cards is still face down.

When one card remains face down

Once you have only one face-down card left, you may choose to draw and discard without swapping or turning over that last card. You can repeat this skip on later turns while it stays face down.

Putting out and finishing a hole

When someone turns over or replaces their final face-down card, every other player takes exactly one more turn. Then reveal any remaining face-down cards and score all eight cards in each layout.

Score cards and matched columns

Add the eight face values. A vertical pair (the top card directly above the bottom card) with matching values cancels to zero. A matched Hole-in-One pair is the exception: both cards still count -5, for -10 total.

  • Two vertical pairs of the same value: those four cards score 0, plus a -10 bonus.
  • Three pairs of the same value: those six cards score 0, plus a -15 bonus.
  • Four pairs of the same value: all eight cards score 0, plus a -20 bonus.
  • Four Hole-in-Ones arranged as two matching columns score -20 for the cards, then receive the -10 bonus, for -30.

Tip: turn matched sets face down while adding the remaining values so you don’t count them twice.

Start the next hole

The player to the left of the previous dealer shuffles and deals the next hand. Everyone tees off again, and the player to the new dealer’s left starts.

End of the game and ties

After the ninth hole, compare the totals for all nine holes. The lowest total wins. Players tied for the lowest score may settle it with a sudden-death playoff.
