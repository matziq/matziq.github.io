How scoring works here (read once)
  • Results show percent correct, by domain and by objective group. Nothing on this site converts that to a Microsoft scaled score — Microsoft does not publish a conversion, and 700 on its 1–1,000 scale is not the same as 70%.
  • The 80% readiness target is this site’s study heuristic for original questions. It is a signal to book or keep your date, not a prediction.
  • Real exams can include case studies, drag-and-drop, build-list, and other formats. These practice sets use single-answer multiple choice so you can drill the decisions behind those formats.
  • Take the official free practice assessment on Microsoft Learn too — it is the best guide to the real question style.
