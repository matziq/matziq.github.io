Study plan and calendar

Built from your exam dates, study days, and weekly hours. About 70% of sessions teach the official objectives in proportion to domain weight; the rest are timed practice exams, review, and a final taper before each exam.

The .ics file contains all-day events for every study session and milestone, so it imports cleanly into Outlook, Google Calendar, or Apple Calendar without time-zone shifts. Re-download it after you change dates.

How the plan is built
  • Window A (AB-620): from your plan start date to the day before AB-620. Window B (AB-100): from the day after AB-620 to the day before AB-100.
  • Each objective group gets at least one session; extra sessions go to groups in proportion to the midpoint of the official domain weight. Multi-session groups move through learn → lab → drill.
  • When there is room, a domain review session follows each domain. Each window then ends with three timed practice exams, review sessions, and a taper day.
  • Session length is your weekly hours divided by your study days. If a window is too short, groups are combined, and the settings panel warns you.
  • Checkmarks are stored by date and exam. If you move dates, some checked work may move to different days — that is expected.
Missed a day?

Do not try to double up. Do the oldest unchecked session next, and if you fall more than a week behind, add a study day in the settings or push the exam date (keeping the retake buffer).
