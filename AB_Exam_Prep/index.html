Why AB-620 first, then AB-100
  • The credential math. The Agentic AI Business Solutions Architect Expert certification requires passing AB-100 and holding at least one qualifying associate certification. AB-620 (Microsoft Certified: AI Agent Builder Associate) is one of the qualifying associates listed on the AB-100 page — along with others such as PL-200, PL-400, MB-280, and AI-103. If you do not already hold one, passing AB-620 first means the expert credential can be granted as soon as you pass AB-100.
  • Builder before architect. AB-620 is hands-on Copilot Studio: agent flows, topics, knowledge, tools, MCP, multi-agent, evaluation, and ALM. AB-100 asks you to choose between those same capabilities (and Dynamics 365, Microsoft 365 Copilot, and Foundry options) as an architect. Building first makes architect trade-offs concrete.
  • Retake room. Microsoft’s retake policy requires 24 hours after a first failed attempt and 14 days between later attempts. Putting the harder, expert-level exam last with a buffer before March 30 leaves room to recover.
Exam vs credential

Passing an exam and earning the credential are different. You can sit AB-100 before you hold a qualifying associate, but the Expert certification is only awarded once both requirements are met. Check the certification page for the current list.

Deadline, retake policy, and your buffer

Your goal is both exams passed before March 30, 2027, so the last usable exam day is Monday, March 29, 2027.

  • First failed attempt: wait at least 24 hours before retaking.
  • After that: wait at least 14 days between attempts.
  • No more than five attempts of the same exam in 12 months. Each retake is paid unless you have a voucher or offer that covers it.

With the default AB-100 date of March 9, an immediate retake could happen March 10 and a second retake as early as March 24 — both before the deadline. The settings panel above recalculates this for your dates and warns you when the buffer gets thin.

Plan for availability, not just policy

Policy minimums are not seat availability. Test center and online proctoring slots can fill up, so book your first attempt early and check retake slots the week before your exam.
