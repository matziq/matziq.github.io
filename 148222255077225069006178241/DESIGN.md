# Context, Not Caricature — Design System

## Direction

The page behaves like an evidence desk rather than a campaign site. It uses split tracks, annotated records, and visible source paths to let readers hold competing truths without flattening moral distinctions.

## Visual language

- **Ink:** `#132a35` for structure, authority, and high-contrast reading.
- **Mineral blue:** `#1f6072` for the Israeli perspective and evidence controls.
- **Clay:** `#b34f35` for the Palestinian perspective, warnings, and the closing argument.
- **Paper:** `#f4f3ed` with a restrained grid texture; content surfaces use `#fffefa`.
- **Gold:** `#cc9c42` for focus, balance points, and decisive actions.
- Condensed system display faces carry major arguments; a workhorse system sans carries evidence and long-form copy.
- Corners remain square. Depth uses offset shadows, not floating glass.

## Composition

The opening is a thesis paired with a literal two-sided axis. Long sections alternate between open paper, dense evidence tables, split perspective fields, and dark legal analysis. Rules and alignment do more work than containers.

## Interaction

- Timeline filters preserve chronology while narrowing the reading path.
- Perspective controls switch between paired and focused views.
- Claims and FAQs use native disclosure controls.
- Search uses a curated topic index and suggested terms.
- Motion is limited to navigation, disclosure, search feedback, and the opening balance language.

## Accessibility

The page uses semantic landmarks, native controls, visible focus, reduced-motion support, readable measures, print styles, mobile navigation, and contrast designed to meet WCAG AA.
