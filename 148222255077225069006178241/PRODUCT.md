# Product Context

## Purpose

Context, Not Caricature is a private-link educational guide for readers encountering reductive claims about the Israeli-Palestinian conflict. It helps a reader distinguish history, perspective, evidence, intent, consequence, and international law without requiring agreement with a political camp.

## Audience and job

The audience is a general reader in a difficult conversation who needs a concise map of the conflict, direct paths to reputable sources, and language precise enough to reject dehumanizing shortcuts.

## Core commitments

- Israeli and Palestinian attachment, rights, security, and suffering are treated as real.
- Deliberate attacks on civilians are distinguished from incidental civilian harm without treating the latter as automatically lawful.
- Disputed claims are labeled and source limitations are visible.
- No section acts as legal adjudication or substitutes for current reporting.
- The page is unlisted and requests no indexing, but it does not claim cryptographic privacy.
