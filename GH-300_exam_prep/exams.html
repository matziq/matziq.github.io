The Practice Exams

Three full-length practice exams, one for each checkpoint in your plan. Each one is this site’s format: 60 questions in 100 minutes, weighted across the GH-300 domains.

Take these properly or do not take them at all

A practice exam with notes open tells you nothing. Sit down, close every tab, start the clock, and do not stop. The score is a readiness signal, not an official Microsoft score.

How the score is estimated

Microsoft reports GH-300 on a 1 to 1000 scale and passes at 700. Microsoft has not published the raw-to-scaled conversion, and the real exam weights questions by difficulty.

This site shows an estimated scaled score so you can track readiness. Do not read it as an official prediction.
