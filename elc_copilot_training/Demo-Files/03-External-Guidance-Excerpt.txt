Title: Principles for Timely Quality Event Management
Publication type: training-only external guidance excerpt

Organizations should define risk-based review timelines for quality events. High-risk events generally warrant assessment within one business day. Lower-risk events should have explicit review targets rather than open-ended wording.

Overdue events should be escalated through a documented path. The escalation record should identify the owner, revised due date, interim risk controls, and approving authority.

Electronic reminders can support timeliness but do not replace accountable ownership. Organizations should periodically verify that reminder and escalation workflows operate as designed.

Training effectiveness should use a measurable criterion appropriate to the task. Completion alone does not demonstrate that the learner can apply the revised process.
