FICTIONAL TRAINING EXERCISE — CHOOSE ONE WEAK PROMPT

1. "Summarize the quality issue."
Desired outcome: A decision-ready status with evidence, owners, and next steps.

2. "Analyze this spreadsheet."
Desired outcome: A defensible trend analysis that flags data-quality limitations.

3. "Make a PowerPoint about the investigation."
Desired outcome: A ten-slide cross-functional review grounded only in the investigation brief.

4. "Write an update for leadership."
Desired outcome: A concise update that separates confirmed facts, assumptions, risks, and asks.

Paste one weak prompt and its desired outcome into Demo 3. Do not add real customer or employee information.
