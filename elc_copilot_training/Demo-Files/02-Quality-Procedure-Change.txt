FICTIONAL TRAINING SOURCE — PROCEDURE CHANGE
Document: QP-104, Review and Escalation of Quality Events
Draft revision: 7
Effective date: Proposed October 15, 2026

1. Purpose
This revision introduces risk-based review timing and a documented escalation path for overdue quality events.

2. Scope
Applies to manufacturing, packaging, and laboratory quality events recorded at the Riverton, Delft, and Kaipara training sites.

3. Definitions
Critical event: an event with potential impact to patient or consumer safety, regulatory compliance, or released-product integrity.
Major event: an event that may affect product quality or process control but does not meet the Critical definition.
Business day: Monday through Friday, excluding local site holidays.

4. Requirements
4.1 Critical events must receive an initial Quality assessment within 1 business day of record creation.
4.2 Major events must receive an initial Quality assessment within 3 business days.
4.3 Minor events should be reviewed promptly.
4.4 Event owners must update the record periodically while an investigation remains open.
4.5 Events open longer than 30 calendar days require a documented recovery plan.
4.6 Events open longer than 60 calendar days require escalation to the site Quality lead.
4.7 Extensions beyond 90 calendar days require approval from the site Quality lead and a documented risk assessment.
4.8 Supporting evidence should be attached where feasible.

5. New workflow
The event owner drafts the assessment. A Quality reviewer confirms classification, assigns due dates, and documents required actions. The site Quality lead reviews escalations. The system sends reminders 5 calendar days before a due date and again on the due date.

6. Records
The event record, attachments, approvals, recovery plans, and extension decisions are retained according to the applicable site retention schedule.

7. Training
Affected users must complete training before the effective date. Training effectiveness will be checked as appropriate.

8. Open questions in this draft

- Who owns reminder failures?
- What evidence is sufficient under 4.8?
